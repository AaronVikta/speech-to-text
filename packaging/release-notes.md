## Download

Pick the file for your computer from **Assets** below:

| Your computer | Download this | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** | `RecordingToWord-Windows-Setup.exe` | Double-click it and follow the installer. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** → **Run anyway**. |
| **Mac** | `RecordingToWord-Mac.dmg` | Open it and drag **Recording to Word** into **Applications**. The first time, right-click the app → **Open** → **Open**. |
| **Linux** | `RecordingToWord-Linux.AppImage` | Right-click → **Properties** → allow running as a program, then double-click it. |

**Ignore the "Source code" files**: those are for developers.

### How to use it
1. Open **Recording to Word**.
2. Drag your recording onto the window, or click **Choose file**.
3. Click **Generate Word document** and choose where to save it.

The first time, the app downloads its speech model (a few hundred MB), so it needs internet once. After that it works offline.
